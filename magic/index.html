Handling

  1. First spectator calls a 3- or 4-digit number. Type it, press +.
  2. Second spectator calls another. Type it, press =. Say the sum aloud.
  3. Press and hold × for half a second. The key turns white — that is the only thing to remember. A normal tap of × just multiplies.
  4. Phone face down. Third spectator taps, as many times as she likes. Nine is the most it ever needs; extras are ignored.
  5. Take the phone back, press and hold anywhere to release the keypad, then =.

Turn the phone sideways for the reveal — the full number and the whole sum sit at readable size in landscape. Rotating changes nothing about the arithmetic and is safe at any point.

Hold the clock icon, top left, for the rehearsal readout. Tap anywhere to close this.
